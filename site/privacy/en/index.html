Nekan privacy policy

Effective 2026-08-10 · Nekan

Nothing is collected unless you sign in. Nekan works completely without an account, and in that case your tasks only ever live in a file on your own computer. Everything below applies only when you have signed in to sync across devices.

1. What is collected, and why

Data Purpose Collected when
Email address Identifying the account and showing you are signed in You sign in with a Google account
User identifier (UUID) Telling your tasks apart from everyone else's The account is created
The name and profile picture on your Google account Google hands these over with the account details at sign-in and the authentication server keeps them. The app neither reads nor writes them and shows them nowhere. You sign in with a Google account
Task content — text, notes, due dates, quadrant, Work/Life side, sort order, and the times it was created, edited, completed, deleted or deleted forever Syncing across your devices You create or change a task while signed in

What is never collected:

  • Phone number, address, date of birth, gender
  • Location, contacts, photos, files
  • Advertising identifiers — there are no ads and no tracking of any kind
  • Usage or analytics data — no analytics tool is built into the app

2. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete your account. Settings (⚙) → Delete account in the app removes the account and the tasks held on the server, and it cannot be undone.
  • When you delete a task forever its content — the text and the note — is erased immediately. A content-free deletion marker (an identifier and a timestamp) stays for 90 days so that a device which has not synced yet cannot bring the task back, and is then removed.
  • Signing out is not deletion. The tasks on your computer and the copy on the server both stay.

3. Processors

Processor What they do Where data is stored
Supabase, Inc. Account authentication, storing and syncing task data Seoul, South Korea region
Google LLC Google account sign-in Per Google's own policy

Beyond those two, nothing is shared with or sold to anyone. Google sign-in only happens if you choose that method yourself.

The records that exist simply because a server was spoken to — access logs, including IP addresses — are kept by those processors and by whoever serves this page, each under their own policy. Nekan neither collects nor uses them.

4. Your rights

  • Access and correction — everything is visible and editable inside the app at any time. No request is needed.
  • Deletion — Settings (⚙) → Delete account does it yourself. If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot reach that screen, use the deletion request page.
  • Withdrawing consent — signing out stops anything further being sent to the server. To remove the copy already there, delete the account.

5. How the data is protected

  • All traffic to and from the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The database enforces that you can only read and write your own rows (Row Level Security). Somebody else's tasks do not come back even if asked for.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored on your computer encrypted through the operating system's secure storage, never in plain text.

6. Children

Nekan is not directed at children under 14, and their personal data is not knowingly collected.

7. Changes to this policy

If this changes, this page is updated and the effective date above is changed with it. Significant changes are also announced inside the app.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, write to nekan.support@gmail.com.
